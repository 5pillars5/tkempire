What each number means

Observed change: actual log return in basis points (1 bp = 0.01%) over approximately 20 seconds. Latest speed: price-change rate in basis points per second during the newest interval. Acceleration: difference between recent and earlier speed, divided by the time between the interval midpoints. None of these is a future-price forecast.

Data gates: Feed source, timestamp and market must match the allowlist. Need at least three samples with a valid observed window. Missing, delayed or irregular data displays COLLECTING or STALE, not a signal. Data collected in one browser session is not a persistent backtest.
