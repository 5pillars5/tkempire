TK Empire · Recovery Lab V1

Turn the loss into a measurable plan.

Recovery Lab helps you measure drawdown, define safety boundaries, compare recovery scenarios, and track progress over time. It is a planning tool — not a promise that a loss can be recovered on a schedule.

Important: The calculator uses assumptions you enter. It does not predict market returns, recommend trades, or authorize Genesis execution. Trading can create additional losses.
