RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

Flow is not the same thing as probability.

Buyer consensus counts distinct wallets net buying the same outcome. Capital flow shows buying minus selling among observed fills. Acceleration compares recent 15-minute net flow with the previous interval.

A high pressure ratio can reflect one large buyer, a hedge, incomplete sampling, or an already-expensive entry. It does not imply the event will occur.

For genuine probability estimates, Genesis needs independently verified outcomes, fixed-time snapshots, actual entry prices, fees, and out-of-sample calibration. That model is not deployed.
