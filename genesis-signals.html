What the labels mean

Qualified signal: Genesis passed its admission checks, but that does not mean a position opened. Verified open paper position: its opening was corroborated by the canonical paper ledger. Signal trigger: the planned price level, not a verified exchange fill. Stop loss and target: research levels, not guaranteed executions. Gross unrealized movement: paper entry versus current indicative mid, direction-aware, per coin and percent; not total trade P&L. Closed trade: a canonical close with recorded exit, close timestamp and realized outcome in its explicitly labeled paper accounting units; no result is guessed from market prices. The page is read-only, not a trading terminal, and it cannot place orders.
