HOW THE RATES REALLY WORK

The price moves for new members. Your maximum stays protected.

Genesis performance is reviewed using independently verified, closed trade results. Prices do not change after each trade or each winning streak. Everyone gets the same features; the schedule changes only what new subscribers may pay and whether current subscribers qualify for a lower renewal.

01 · When you join

Your original rate becomes your ceiling

After your first confirmed payment, that monthly price is your personal maximum for the membership while you stay eligible.

02 · If Genesis performs better

New subscribers may pay more

Higher rates require sustained verified results, positive net performance and risk checks. You never pay above your original maximum.

03 · If Genesis performs worse

Your renewal may drop

When a verified review lowers the public rate, you pay that lower rate at your next renewal. Your original maximum remains yours if results recover.

How does Genesis qualify for a price change?

The staged monitoring design reviews roughly 28–31 days of independently reconciled closed PAPER trades, with at least 100 decisive wins/losses. An increase requires two separate qualifying reviews about a week apart, positive verified net P&L after fees, and approved risk checks. A lower band can be proposed after one verified review. Missing or unverified evidence means no new rate decision. These review thresholds and automated billing have not been activated or approved for production.
