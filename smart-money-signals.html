How this works

The scanner samples up to eight currently profitable, high-volume wallets from the public weekly crypto leaderboard, then scans each wallet's latest public trade events. It detects BUY and SELL observations above $250, unusually large trades relative to each wallet's sampled trade history, three-wallet same-market consensus within 30 minutes, and a possible opposing-outcome reversal. Repeated observations use stable event IDs. Signals older than 15 minutes are historical observations, not fresh alerts.
Browser notifications work only while this tab is open and permission is granted. There is no always-on backend scheduler, Telegram alert delivery, persistent paper trading, or automatic copy trading in this version.
Signals are withheld when source data cannot be validated. A failed source or unavailable wallet history is shown as a degraded feed, not as successful monitoring. No Genesis VPS configuration, execution service, maintenance gate or exchange wallet is accessed.
